# Candidates — Say What Actually Broke

Thirteen candidate error lines were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the winner then defended its place against three fresh challengers in a revision round.

**Final winner (151 chars):** Install failed at 99%: your disk is full because our last three updates never deleted themselves. Free up 2 GB, or delete this app. We will understand.

**Runner-up (167 chars):** You deleted 4,000 photos. We asked "Are you sure?" in the same grey box we use for everything, so you clicked yes out of habit. They are in Trash for 30 days. Breathe.
